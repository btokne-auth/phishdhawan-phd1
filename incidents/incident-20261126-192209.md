# Incident: phishing wave imitating lumenbank.ph

- **Logged:** 2026-11-26 19:22:09
- **Wave:** 9 phishing emails between 09:14 and 09:47
- **Fake domains:** lumen-bank-aid.com, lumenbamk.ph, lumenbank-alerts.co, lumenbank-cares.com, lumenbank-secure.net, lumenbank-testcenter.com, lumenbank-verify.com, lumenbnk.ph
- **Model:** openai/gpt-oss-120b
- **First protection:** 3.3s after the wave was found

## PHD1's assessment

A wave of phishing emails is impersonating lumenbank.ph using multiple fake domains and urgent financial assistance claims. Lumen Bank customers are at risk of credential theft and fraud.
