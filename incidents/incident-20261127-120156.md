# Incident: phishing wave imitating lumenbank.ph

- **Logged:** 2026-11-27 12:01:56
- **Wave:** 9 phishing emails between 09:14 and 09:47
- **Fake domains:** lumen-bank-aid.com, lumenbamk.ph, lumenbank-alerts.co, lumenbank-cares.com, lumenbank-secure.net, lumenbank-testcenter.com, lumenbank-verify.com, lumenbnk.ph

## PHD1's assessment

A wave of phishing emails is impersonating lumenbank.ph using fake domains and urgent financial assistance claims. Lumenbank customers and anyone receiving these emails are at risk of credential theft.
