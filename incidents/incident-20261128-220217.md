# Incident: phishing wave imitating PesoPay

- **Logged:** 2026-11-28 22:02:17
- **Wave:** 5 phishing emails between 10:02 and 10:31
- **Fake domains:** pesopay-kyc.net, pesopay-promo.site, pesopay-secure.co, pesopay-update.xyz, pesopay-wallet.com
- **Model:** openai/gpt-oss-120b
- **First protection:** 2.8s after the wave was found

## PHD1's assessment

A wave of phishing emails impersonating PesoPay is targeting its users with fake domains and urgent wallet-related subjects.

## Lures seen

- 10:02 `K7` Your PesoPay wallet will be suspended
- 10:09 `K8` Action needed: verify your wallet
- 10:15 `K9` Unusual login on your wallet
- 10:24 `K10` You received 500 PHP cashback
- 10:31 `K11` Last notice: update your wallet today

## Actions

| Time | Who | Action | Result | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 22:02:19 | PHD1 | switched ON phishing_warning_banner | low risk, did it alone | Warn recipients that the email pretends to be from PesoPay, reducing the chance they follow malicious instructions. |
| 22:02:19 | PHD1 | asked to switch ON block_external_links | high risk, waiting for a human | Prevent users from clicking malicious links to the fake domains used in the phishing campaign. |
| 22:02:34 | Neiko | approved block_external_links | switched ON |  |
